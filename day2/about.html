About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight productivity tool designed to help developers and learners quickly capture ideas, daily tasks, and notes without clutter.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Enter the title and details of your note in the form.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save your note.
  3. View and manage your saved notes under the Recent Notes section.

Features

  • Instant note saving with zero lag
  • Clean, minimal interface focused on productivity
  • Keyboard shortcut support for power users

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Save current note
Esc Clear input fields
